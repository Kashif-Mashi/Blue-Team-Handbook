## Story

A thief has stolen your `top_secret.txt` and hidden clues all around your system. Only a true Linux detective can follow the riddles, rebuild the team, protect the evidence, and catch the traitor.

Each riddle leads to one Linux concept—solve wisely, detective.

---

## Mission 1 - Build the Investigation Base

**Clue:**
> "Three rooms I need: one for truth, one for lies, one for stories that never die."

**Your Task:**
Set up your investigation base in your system. Create a safe structure where you can keep suspects, evidence, and reports. Hide a small note in each—you never know which one holds the real clue.

* **Goal:** Build and organize directories and files.

---

## Mission 2 - Form the Secret Team

**Clue:**
> "Two minds are better than one, but only if they wear the same badge."

**Your Task:**
Recruit two trusted agents. Form a private team that only your agents can join. Confirm that each of them proudly wears your team badge.

* **Goal:** Work with users and groups.

---

## Mission 3 - Protect the Evidence

**Clue:**
> "I must see what's mine, they may see what's theirs, but none shall touch what isn't theirs."

**Your Task:**
Secure your evidence so only you can edit it, your team can only peek, and outsiders are blind to it. Check that the locks are working properly.

* **Goal:** Use file permissions wisely (`r`, `w`, `x` concepts).

---

## Mission 4 - Pass the Torch

**Clue:**
> "A clue is only useful when it belongs to the right hands."

**Your Task:**
One of your agents needs to own the crucial evidence to reveal the truth. Pass the responsibility and the rights to them. Switch places and see if they can uncover what you couldn't.

* **Goal:** Change ownership and verify access.

---

## Mission 5 - The Traitor Revealed

**Clue:**
> "Power in the wrong hands turns friends into foes."

**Your Task:**
Investigate who in your system holds forbidden powers. If one of your agents has authority they shouldn't, strip it before it's too late. Then write a final report—justice must be documented.

* **Goal:** Discover and manage sudo privileges.
